# Contributing to Awesome AI Agent Security

Thanks for helping build the best curated resource for AI-agent, LLM, and MCP security. Quality over quantity — this list is opinionated on purpose.

## What belongs here

Resources about the **security of AI agents and LLM-integrated applications**: attacks (prompt injection, jailbreaks, agent hijacking, tool poisoning), defenses (guardrails, scanners, detectors), research papers, hands-on labs, real-world incidents, and people worth following.

## Adding an item

1. Fork and edit `README.md`.
2. Add your entry to the most appropriate section, in this exact format:
   ```
   - [Name](https://link) — One concise sentence on what it is and why it matters.
   ```
3. Keep the sentence factual and specific. No marketing fluff ("blazing-fast revolutionary…").
4. Put items in a sensible order within a section (foundational/most-important first).
5. Open a pull request with a title like `Add <Name> to <Section>`.

## Quality bar (PRs are reviewed against this)

- **Real and live.** The link must resolve and the resource must exist.
- **On-topic.** It must be about *security* of AI agents/LLMs, not general AI.
- **Not redundant.** Don't add a near-duplicate of an existing entry.
- **Notable.** Prefer authoritative sources, actively-maintained tools, and cited research. Personal blog posts are welcome if they contain genuinely useful, original analysis.
- **Disclose affiliation.** If it's your own project/company, say so in the PR. Self-promotion is fine if the resource is genuinely good.

## Formatting rules

- One entry per line, alphabetical or importance-ordered within a subsection.
- Use `—` (em dash) between the link and the description.
- No trailing period is fine; be consistent within a section.
- Commercial products are allowed but should be marked `(commercial)`.

## Suggesting a new section

Open an issue first so we can discuss where it fits. The taxonomy is deliberate.
